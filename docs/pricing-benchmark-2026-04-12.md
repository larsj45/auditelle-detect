# Pricing benchmark — 2026-04-12

Goal: keep VeriTexto positioned as the practical Brazilian alternative to Turnitin while preserving a clear entry point for professor, school, and university buyers.

## Official pricing anchors checked

### Turnitin

- Turnitin still routes buyers through demo/contact flows and institutional sales rather than a transparent self-serve plan page.
- Official references:
  - [Turnitin demo request](https://go.turnitin.com/DemoRequest_en_int)
  - [Turnitin Feedback Studio](https://www.turnitin.com/products/feedback-studio)

Implication: for Brazil, "compra simples em reais" is a strong positioning angle versus institution-only procurement.

### GPTZero

- GPTZero publicly frames its offer as four tiers: Basic, Essential, Premium, and Professional.
- Official reference:
  - [GPTZero pricing overview](https://gptzero.me/news/pricing)

Implication: GPTZero is a transparent SaaS benchmark, but it is still sold in USD and not packaged around Brazilian academic procurement.

### Originality.ai

- Originality.ai publicly exposes a pay-as-you-go option and recurring plans on its pricing page.
- Official reference:
  - [Originality.ai pricing](https://originality.ai/pricing)

Implication: Originality.ai is the clearest self-serve price anchor, but it is also USD-priced and not tailored to professor/school/university segmentation in Brazil.

## Positioning recommendation for VeriTexto BR

1. Professor
   - Primary offer: monthly subscription with direct checkout.
   - Message: "assine e use sozinho".

2. Escola
   - Primary offer: higher-volume subscription with multiple users.
   - Message: "padronize a correção entre docentes".

3. Universidade
   - Primary offer: contact-led institutional proposal.
   - Message: "implantação, integração e volume customizado".

4. Entry offer
   - Keep pay-per-scan visible as a low-friction paid entry point.
   - Use it as the bridge between free signup and recurring subscription.

## Why this matters

- Turnitin is easier to attack on procurement friction than on feature-by-feature messaging alone.
- GPTZero and Originality.ai anchor expectations for transparent self-serve pricing, but neither solves BRL checkout + Portuguese support + local institutional packaging.
- The homepage and campaign should therefore sell three things at once:
  - easier purchase,
  - clearer segmentation,
  - Brazilian-localized checkout and support.
